      title: Running experiments inside a computer
      text: |
        Many phenomena in nature and in society are difficult to study by experiment. Even when an experiment is possible, it may require a large facility and considerable cost, so that examining the phenomenon under a range of conditions becomes impractical. The motion of solar flares and the solar wind, the spread of smoke in a fire, and the evacuation behavior and flow of people are examples of such phenomena.

        This is where computer simulation is used. By reproducing a phenomenon in a simulation, we are able to analyze what experiment alone cannot reach, and to clarify the mechanism behind it.

        In our group, we aim to understand the mechanisms of complex phenomena through simulation research.
